How to use

Make your Fruit Punch ritual drink. Start with a month-long course. Drink FLO three times a week. A simple ritual for self-regulation. As easy as your morning coffee.

Recommended serving
+ Frequency: Three times a week.
+ Starting serving: Mix ¼ scoop (1.875 g) with 250 ml of water. Increase the serving according to your tolerance.
+ Maximum daily serving: Do not exceed 2 scoops (15 g) per day.

Pack contents
180 g / 12 servings of 15 g
1 pack / 4 weeks of ritual / 3 servings per week.

carefully formulated

FLO is a carefully formulated blend
of 18 ingredients.
Amounts per 15 g serving:

Lion’s Mane Extract

An extract from the fruiting body of the mushroom known as lion’s mane. Its effects on cognitive function are being studied, but findings from human research remain limited and inconclusive.

L-Tyrosine

An amino acid used by the body to build proteins and produce dopamine and noradrenaline. It is also a precursor to thyroid hormones.

Ashwagandha Extract

A plant extract containing withanolides.
Ashwagandha is studied in relation to stress and sleep, with results depending on the
extract, dose and pattern of use.

Glycine

An amino acid naturally present in the body. It contributes to the formation of proteins, including collagen, and to signalling in the nervous system.

Magnesium Citrate

Contributes to the normal functioning of
the nervous system and muscles, and to the
reduction of tiredness and fatigue. FLO
contains magnesium in the form of magnesium citrate.

Guarana Extract

A plant-based source of caffeine, which
increases alertness and reduces sleepiness. Its caffeine content should be taken into
account when consuming coffee and other
caffeine-containing products.

Taurine

A compound naturally present in the body,
including the muscles and nervous system.
It plays a role in regulating cell volume
and calcium balance.

Inositol

A compound naturally present in human cells. It participates in signalling within cells.

Choline Bitartrate

A source of choline, which is needed to build cell membranes and produce acetylcholine. Choline contributes to normal lipid
metabolism.

Cordyceps Extract

A fungal ingredient studied in relation to
exercise tolerance and fatigue, among other areas. Findings apply to specific preparations and do not automatically establish the effects of every extract.

Green Tea Extract

Provides L-theanine, catechins and caffeine. L-theanine is an amino acid naturally found in tea leaves.

Potassium Citrate

Potassium contributes to the normal functioning of muscles and the nervous system, and to the maintenance of normal blood pressure. FLO contains potassium in the form of potassium citrate.

Salt

Provides sodium, one of the electrolytes present in the body. Sodium plays a role in fluid balance and the functioning of nerves and muscles.

Bacopa Monnieri Extract

An extract of Bacopa monnieri standardised for its bacoside content. It has primarily been studied in relation to memory with regular use over several weeks.

Niacin / Vitamin B3

Contributes to the normal functioning of the nervous system and normal energy-yielding
metabolism. It also contributes to the
reduction of tiredness and fatigue.

Zinc

Contributes to normal cognitive function and the normal functioning of the immune system. It also contributes to the protection of cells from oxidative stress.

Vitamin B6

Contributes to the normal functioning of
the nervous system and normal psychological
function. It also contributes to normal
protein and glycogen metabolism.

Black Pepper Extract

An extract standardised for its piperine
content. Its presence does not establish
increased absorption of all ingredients in
the formula.
